# Lotus Artwork — preview

Static site (HTML/CSS/JS, no build). Open `index.html` or run `python3 -m http.server 8766`.

Status: PREVIEW. Shop is display-only; no cart, checkout, payment or contact form.
"Portrait 01–05" are working labels. Still needed from the artist: titles, prices,
(optional) medium/size/year, availability, contact and payment method.

Images in `assets/art/` are exact crops of the supplied phone screenshots (no retouching).
Portraits 01–04: crops of phone screenshots. Portrait 04 was trimmed ~165px on the right edge to exclude a phone-UI clock icon.
Portrait 05 was photographed on an easel: cropped to the drawing with a thin paper margin and
perspective-corrected with a four-point transform (no repainting/recolouring); signature preserved.
Logo: pink lotus flower from the Lotus Acupuncture logo with the needles removed (alpha components only, petals unaltered).
Background: assets/green-lotus-bg.jpg is an AI-generated image (lotus flowers), edited from a user-supplied green floral reference.
